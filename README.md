## 👋 Hi, I'm Michelle

#### I am currently a postdoctoral research fellow at Indiana University School of Medicine in the Department of Biostatistics and Health Data Sciences. My research focuses on neural development and the impact of early life environmental abnormalities (drug exposure) and late life cognitive decline (Alzheimer's disease). 

#### My educational background is:
#### 📚 🎓 Bachelor of Arts - Psychology & Spanish - Franklin College 
#### 🔬 🎓 Master of Science - Biotechnology - Indiana University Bloomington
#### 🏛️ 🎓 PhD - Neuroscience & Psychology - Indiana University Bloomington

#### I specialize in:  
#### 🧹 Cleaning, transforming, and organizing raw data
#### 📊 Analyzing and visualizing data using R, Python, and SQL
#### 🔎 Building and deploying predictive models using machine learning techniques 
#### 🗣️ Communicating findings to both technical and non-technical audiences
